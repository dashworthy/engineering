# The entrance contract

All four entrances — `signal`, `triage`, `simplify`, `receiving-code-review` — share one convergence
contract. Each shapes its own context in its own way, but the seam out of an entrance is identical, so
it lives here once.

## Converge on brainstorming, through no gate

Once an entrance has shaped its context and written it into the run, it hands that context to the
shared design dialogue: **invoke `engineering:brainstorming` now.** Everything converges there — there
is no routing table, no quick-fix side door, and **no gate at this entrance seam.** Approval lives at
the downstream gates, never in an entrance or in brainstorming.

## Don't ask permission to proceed

Reporting the shaped context and asking whether to proceed is not a move here. Parking the work with a
"want me to start design?" is not an available option — design is the next act, take it. "Stop" means
stop the entrance's own shaping work (interrogating, isolating, verifying) and do not design, plan, or
build yourself — it is **not** a stop to ask whether to proceed. Once the context is shaped and written
into the run, invoke brainstorming.

## Discovery mining is your own leg — never another entrance

When shaping needs generic requirement-mining — an unclear stakeholder, expected behavior that must be
synthesized, a success criterion the entrance's own beat cannot name — invoke the shared discovery
skill (`engineering:interrogating-requirements`) and drive it in the main thread. That is the
entrance's **own discovery leg**, not a hand-off: **the entrances never invoke one another.**
